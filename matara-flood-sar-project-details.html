Pre- & Post-Flood Analysis Using SAR Imagery

October 8, 2023 Flood Event – Matara District, Sri Lanka

Project Overview

This project analyses the spatial extent and impacts of the October 8, 2023 flood event in Matara District using Synthetic Aperture Radar (SAR) imagery. Pre-flood and post-flood Sentinel-1 data were compared to accurately delineate inundated areas and assess flood impacts under cloud-covered conditions.

Study Area

The study area covers the Matara District in southern Sri Lanka, a region frequently affected by flooding due to intense monsoonal rainfall and the overflow of the Nilwala River. Low-lying floodplains and densely populated urban areas increase the district’s vulnerability to flood hazards.

Data Sources

  • Sentinel-1 SAR imagery (Pre-flood: 26 September 2023)
  • Sentinel-1 SAR imagery (Post-flood: 08 October 2023)
  • Land Use / Land Cover (LULC) datasets
  • Digital Elevation Model (DEM)
  • Infrastructure layers (roads, railways, education centers)

Methodology

  • SAR image preprocessing (radiometric correction, terrain correction, speckle filtering)
  • Threshold-based water and non-water classification
  • Change detection using pre- and post-flood SAR imagery
  • Flood extent delineation through backscatter analysis
  • Overlay analysis with land use and infrastructure layers

Flood Analysis Maps

The maps illustrate hydrological changes before and after the flood event. A significant expansion of water-covered areas is observed along the Nilwala River and surrounding low-lying regions, confirming extensive inundation.

Key Observations

  • Significant floodwater expansion occurred along the Nilwala River floodplain
  • Low-lying agricultural and residential areas were highly vulnerable
  • Flooding extended into built-up areas and transportation corridors
  • SAR imagery enabled reliable flood detection despite cloud cover

Tools & Skills

  • ArcGIS Pro – spatial analysis and flood mapping
  • ENVI – SAR image preprocessing and analysis
  • SAR-based change detection techniques
  • Disaster risk and impact assessment

Conclusion

This project demonstrates the effectiveness of Synthetic Aperture Radar (SAR) data for flood monitoring and disaster assessment. The integration of SAR imagery with GIS analysis enabled accurate flood extent mapping and impact evaluation, providing valuable insights for disaster preparedness, flood mitigation, and infrastructure planning in flood-prone regions.
