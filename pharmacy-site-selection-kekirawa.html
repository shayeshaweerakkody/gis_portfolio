Site Selection for a Pharmacy

GIS-based Analysis Using Model Builder in ArcGIS

Project Overview

This project identifies the most suitable location for a new pharmacy in the Kekirawa DS Division, Sri Lanka. Using ArcGIS Model Builder, spatial analysis incorporates population density, healthcare accessibility, transport networks, competitor locations, and urban proximity.

Study Area

The study area is the Kekirawa Divisional Secretariat Division in Sri Lanka, encompassing urban and rural communities. Key features include settlements, hospitals, government buildings, dispensaries, main roads, lakes, and agricultural areas.

Data Sources

  • DS Division Boundary – Survey Department of Sri Lanka
  • Road Network and Transportation Infrastructure
  • Hospitals, Clinics, and Dispensaries
  • Population Distribution & Demographics
  • Land Use (Agricultural & Water Bodies)
  • City Limit of Kekirawa

Methodology

  • Buffer analysis for settlements, hospitals, roads, dispensaries, and city limits
  • Identification of unsuitable areas (lakes, agricultural areas)
  • Model Builder workflow using Append & Erase tools to combine buffers
  • Spatial suitability mapping to determine optimal pharmacy location
  • Visualization with thematic map, labels, legend, and symbology

Model Builder Workflow

The Model Builder workflow automates geoprocessing steps including buffer creation, feature combination, and suitability assessment to identify the optimal pharmacy site.

Site Selection Map

The map highlights suitable areas for establishing a pharmacy based on population density, accessibility, competitor presence, and urban proximity. Areas in yellow represent the optimal site identified through the GIS analysis.

Key Observations

  • Optimal pharmacy site located within Kekirawa city limit
  • Accessibility analysis ensures proximity to main roads and hospitals
  • Competitor analysis identifies low-saturation areas for new pharmacy
  • Unsuitable areas (lakes, agricultural zones) excluded from selection

Tools & Skills

  • ArcGIS Pro
  • Model Builder for automated geoprocessing
  • Buffer and spatial analysis techniques
  • Thematic map creation and visualization

Conclusion

Using Model Builder in ArcGIS, this project successfully identifies a suitable pharmacy location in Kekirawa DS Division. The analysis integrates multiple spatial criteria, demonstrating GIS as a powerful tool for evidence-based site selection in healthcare planning.

Site Selection for a Pharmacy

GIS-based Site Suitability Analysis in Kekirawa DS Division

Project Overview

This project identifies the optimal location for a new pharmacy in the Kekirawa Divisional Secretariat Division of Sri Lanka. Using GIS-based spatial analysis and Model Builder in ArcGIS, the project integrates multiple criteria including population density, healthcare facility accessibility, road network connectivity, competitor locations, and urban proximity.

Study Area

Kekirawa DS Division is located in the Anuradhapura District, serving both urban and rural populations. The area is a mix of settlements, main roads, agricultural lands, and water bodies, requiring careful analysis to ensure accessible and viable pharmacy placement.

Data Sources

  • DS Division boundary – Survey Department of Sri Lanka
  • Road network and main roads – Survey Department of Sri Lanka
  • Hospitals, clinics, and dispensaries – Survey Department of Sri Lanka
  • Population distribution and settlements – Survey Department of Sri Lanka
  • Agricultural areas and water bodies – Land use datasets

Methodology

  • Data preprocessing and layer organization
  • Defining selection criteria: population, healthcare access, roads, competitors, city limits
  • Buffer analysis for suitable and unsuitable areas
  • Combining buffer results using Append and Erase tools in Model Builder
  • Visualizing optimal pharmacy locations with thematic mapping

Site Selection Map

The map visualizes the most suitable location for the pharmacy (highlighted in yellow), incorporating population density, healthcare accessibility, transportation networks, competitor analysis, and urban proximity.

Criteria Analysis Graph

The graph illustrates the relative importance of selection criteria such as population concentration, road accessibility, proximity to healthcare facilities, competitor presence, and urban center location.

Key Observations

  • High population density areas align with the optimal pharmacy site
  • Proximity to hospitals and clinics ensures accessibility to patients
  • Main roads and city center proximity improve connectivity and visibility
  • Competitor buffer zones reduce market saturation risks
  • Agricultural areas and water bodies are unsuitable for pharmacy placement

Tools & Skills

  • ArcGIS Pro (Model Builder)
  • Buffer and spatial analysis
  • Cartographic mapping and thematic visualization
  • Integration of multiple datasets for decision-making

Conclusion

This project demonstrates the use of GIS-based site selection analysis to identify the most suitable location for a pharmacy in Kekirawa DS Division. By integrating population, healthcare, transportation, and competitor data, the analysis supports informed and evidence-based healthcare infrastructure planning.
