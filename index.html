About Me

Hi! I'm Shayesha Weerakkody, a BSc (Hons) Geographical Information Science (GIS) graduate from the University of Peradeniya. I have strong academic training and hands-on internship experience in spatial analysis, remote sensing, thematic mapping, and geospatial data management.
