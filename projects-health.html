Spatial Distribution of Healthcare Facilities and Accessibility Analysis

Kandy District, Sri Lanka

This project examines the spatial distribution of healthcare facilities and evaluates accessibility patterns to identify underserved regions using GIS-based network analysis techniques.

Data

Healthcare facility locations, road network, population data

Methods

Network analysis, service area mapping, spatial accessibility

Software

ArcGIS Pro, QGIS

Outcome

Accessibility maps highlighting gaps in healthcare coverage

What I Learned: Developed skills in health GIS, network-based accessibility analysis, and spatial interpretation for public health decision-making.
